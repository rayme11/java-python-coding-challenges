# Day 04 — Arrays & Lists

**Time:** 30–60 min | **Branch:** `day-04-arrays-and-lists`

---

## Today's Concept

Fixed-size arrays (Java) vs dynamic lists (Python), in-place operations,
and the two-pointer technique — the single most reusable array pattern in
interviews.

## Goals

By the end of today you should be able to answer, out loud, without notes:

1. Why can't a Java array grow — and what do you use when you need growth?
2. What does "in-place" mean, and why do interviewers ask for it?
3. Walk through reversing an array with two pointers — when do the pointers stop?
4. What's the cost of inserting at the FRONT of an ArrayList/list, and why?
5. In Python, what's the difference between `sort()` and `sorted()`?
